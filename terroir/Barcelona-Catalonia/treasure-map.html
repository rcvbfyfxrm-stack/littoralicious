BARCELONA

Bread, oil, tomato — everything else is interpretation

A printable A4 of the must-do Barcelona — only the experiences you can have here and nowhere else. No filler, no generic. Tick a box; the mark stays until you reset.

Treasure I✕
Bomba origin at La Cova Fumada
Barceloneta · since 1944 · cash only
The bomba — fried potato bomb stuffed with meat ragù, two sauces — was invented at this barceloneta tasca in 1944. No sign on the door, chalk menu on butcher's paper, three generations of fishermen-and-dockers. Lunch only, closed Sun. Eat it where it was born.
Treasure II✕
L'hora del vermut at Quimet i Quimet
Poble Sec · since 1914 · 6 m² standing
Five generations behind the same six-square-metre counter since 1914. Walls floor-to-ceiling with preserved-fish tins. Sunday 12:30: vermut on the rocks + the canonical montadito (anchovy + foie gras + black-truffle honey, salmon + yogurt + truffle). The Catalan ritual.
Treasure III✕
Bunkers del Carmel sunset
Carmel hill · free · 360°
A 1937 anti-aircraft battery at the top of Carmel hill — concrete platforms, no rails, full 360° over the city, the Sagrada Família and the Mediterranean. Free. Locals climb it Sunday with cava and bocadillos. Bring a bottle, watch the city go gold.
