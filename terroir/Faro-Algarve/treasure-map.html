FARO

Ria Formosa, clams, salt — the Algarve you don't see
Algarve · Portugal · 7 May 2026 · Amêijoa-boa peak

A printable A4 of the must-do Faro — only the experiences you can have here and nowhere else. No filler, no generic. Tick a box; the mark stays until you reset.

Treasure I✕
Cataplana de amêijoas
Tasca do Ricky · 4 h ahead
The hammered-copper pot opened at the table — Ria-Formosa clams, Alentejan pork, carolino rice, coriander, white wine. Order opened in front of you, otherwise it's a stew.
Treasure II✕
Estamine — Ilha Deserta
11:00 ferry from Faro marina
A solar-powered eco-restaurant on an uninhabited barrier sandbar — 7 km of beach, no roads, no shops. Razor clams, oysters, daily catch. The most singular lunch in southern Portugal.
Treasure III✕
Negra Mole at Morgado do Quintão
Lagoa · book 48 h ahead
A family quinta since 1810; Joana Maçanita's revival of the indigenous Algarve grape under a 2,000-year-old olive tree. Lunch at the family table, around the house wines.
