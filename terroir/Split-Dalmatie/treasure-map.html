SPLIT

Stone, wind, pošip — the Dalmatia that lives within
Dalmatia · Croatia · 7 May 2026 · Sudamja

A printable A4 of the must-do Split — only the experiences you can have here and nowhere else. No filler, no generic. Tick a box; the mark stays until you reset.

Treasure I✕
Pašticada with home-made gnocchi
Apetit · upstairs · since 1981
Beef larded with pork fat, marinated 24 h in vinegar, braised 4 h with prunes and Prošek. Brown-leather sauce on dense Croatian gnocchi. The Sunday-feast dish — and today's Sudamja plate.
Treasure II✕
Octopus peka under the iron bell
Konoba Matejuška · 24h ahead
Iron bell heated white on embers over octopus, potatoes, rosemary; covered with red ash 2 hours. Probably Illyrian — predates Romanization. Matejuška's stone-house version is the institution.
Treasure III✕
Three Pelješac terroirs side by side
Paradox Wine & Cheese · Centre
Plavac across Dingač (south, near-vertical), Postup (north, structured) and the broader slope. Three glasses, one grape, three readings — the Croatian wine education in 20 minutes.
